About Roy

I've loved homes for as long as I can remember.

My goal in life is simple: to put smiles on people's faces, and to help them move from one chapter to the next.

I'm Roy Quick, a Realtor® with LUXRE Realty. I live and work in San Clemente and serve clients across Southern California. Before moving to the real estate side, I worked on the finance side of the transaction, so I understand how deals come together and where they can fall apart.

I believe there's never a bad time to be in real estate. Whether the market is up or down, people still need a trusted guide. I especially enjoy working with investors, and with homeowners whose listing didn't sell the first time around. Those are the clients who need someone willing to work harder and think differently.

I'm young, educated, and ready to do whatever it takes for my clients. I want to go above and beyond, and build relationships that last long after closing day.
